### Install AMS to replace the mainboard

Remove the original AMS power mainboard and install the AMS replacement mainboard included in the package. When securing the replacement mainboard, place a nylon washer on the nut side to prevent damage to the mainboard from over-tightening.

Put the original main board back into the anti-static bag for proper storage.

### Arrange the power supply wires for the control module

Plug the power supply cable into the control module, tuck it into the cable management slot on the back, and attach one piece of double-sided tape to the middle-lower part of the module.

<ImageView
    src="/assets/a3b5f0d2-a4cc-4333-b675-a2b41ccbde57"
    alt=""
    width="50%"
/>

Peel off the release liner of the double-sided tape, route the power supply cable under the roller, and temporarily secure the control module in the middle groove of the AMS using the double-sided tape.

<ImageView
    src="/assets/ea4367f3-fc9f-41b2-a85f-d07665d39734"
    alt=""
    width="50%"
/>

Pass the power supply cable through the AMS via the middle hole, and use acetate cloth tape to secure the protruding wiring tightly to the AMS housing so that it does not interfere with the rotation of the roller.

<ImageView
    src="/assets/96ca785b-f483-4ce8-8782-e5457e79d19c"
    alt=""
    width="48%"
/>

Fix the power supply cable with cable ties or acetate cloth at the middle-lower position on the back of the housing as shown in the reference diagram.

<ImageView

Note

Cable routing must not intrude into the AMS gear set or other moving component areas to prevent cable pinching, cable abrasion or pulling during use.

Tip

The three output ports on the control module have identical functions; any two of them can be selected.

Peel off the release liner of the double-sided adhesive tape, and secure the control module and two output modules to their corresponding positions on the AMS main frame.
